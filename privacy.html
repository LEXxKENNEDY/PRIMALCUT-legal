PrimalCut — Privacy Policy

PrimalCut by LexKennedy | Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how PrimalCut, a desktop application for video clipping, caption preparation, and social media publishing, handles information when users interact with its features.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the features used and the permissions granted, PrimalCut may access:

  • Videos and media files selected by the user.
  • Captions, titles, hashtags, and publishing settings.
  • Account identification and authorization information provided by supported platforms.
  • Information needed to submit and check video uploads.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the functions requested by the user, including preparing clips, generating publication text, connecting authorized social media accounts, and submitting videos for publication.

4. Social Media Integrations

PrimalCut may support integrations with YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, depending on the application version and available platform permissions.

Account connections use authorization mechanisms provided by the relevant platforms. PrimalCut requests access only to features necessary for its supported functionality.

When a user chooses to publish a video, the selected media and associated publication information are sent to the relevant platform through its supported publishing interface.

5. Authorization and Account Access

Users decide whether to authorize PrimalCut to access their supported accounts. The permissions requested are displayed during the platform authorization process.

Users can revoke previously granted permissions through the relevant platform's account or security settings.

6. Information Storage and Security

PrimalCut may store application preferences, media references, publication information, and account authorization data as required by its features.

Users should protect their computers and account credentials against unauthorized access. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

7. Data Sharing

Information necessary to complete a user-requested publication is transmitted to the selected social media platform. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies.

This policy does not authorize the sale of users' personal information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may delete locally stored media and publication files using their operating system.

Removing the application does not automatically delete videos previously published on third-party platforms. Users must manage published content through the relevant platform.

Users may also revoke PrimalCut's account access through the platform's authorization settings.

9. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Connected platforms maintain their own privacy practices. Users are encouraged to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Instagram Privacy Policy

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to PrimalCut's features, integrations, or data practices. Updates will be published on this page.
